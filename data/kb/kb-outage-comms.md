# Outages and incident communications

When a major service is down, IT tells staff through set channels so nobody needs to ring the helpdesk and add to the load.

## Where to check first

1. **Status page:** `status.acme.example` shows the state of email, Teams, VPN, the S: drive, printing and the helpdesk portal.
2. **Teams channel:** `IT Status` in the Acme team. Updates are posted here first.
3. **Email:** major incidents are also sent to all staff from `it-alerts@acme.example`. Check it is from that address.

If the status page shows a problem, **do not raise a ticket**. Your report is already counted, and tickets raised for a known outage are closed together when it ends.

## What an update contains

- What is affected and since when
- Whether there is a workaround (for example "use Outlook web")
- When the next update will come. For P1 incidents it is every 30 minutes.

## If it is not listed

If something is down and not on the status page, ring ext. 4000 (08:00 to 18:00). Tell the helpdesk how many people are affected and your location. If it affects a whole office or many people, it becomes a P1 or P2 (see the SLA article).

## Planned maintenance

Planned work is announced at least **5 working days** ahead, usually on weekends. The status page shows maintenance windows.

## After an incident

For P1 incidents, IT posts a short summary of the cause and fix within 3 working days.

## Power or building problems

For a power cut or a network cable fault, contact Facilities on ext. 3000, not IT.
