# Equipment requests, replacement and returns

This article explains how to get new IT equipment, replace something that is broken or lost, and return equipment you no longer need. All company equipment stays the property of Acme Ltd and is tracked by asset tag (the white label on the underside or back of the device).

## Requesting new equipment

1. Raise a ticket in the helpdesk portal with the category **Equipment Request**.
2. State what you need, why you need it, and whether it is a one-off or a permanent change.
3. Your line manager receives an approval request by email and has 3 working days to approve it. Requests with no response are escalated to the department head.
4. Standard items from the catalogue (laptop, 24" monitor, docking station, headset, wireless mouse and keyboard) are normally delivered within 5 working days after approval. Non-standard items need a quote from Procurement and can take up to 15 working days.
5. Equipment over £500 also needs Finance sign-off, which is requested automatically.

## Replacing broken equipment

- Report the fault through the helpdesk portal before doing anything else. Do not attempt repairs yourself or open the device.
- If the device is still usable, keep working on it until the replacement arrives. IT will arrange a swap and move your data.
- Broken accessories (mouse, keyboard, headset) can be exchanged at the IT desk, floor 1, Monday to Friday 09:00 to 16:00, without a manager approval. Bring the old item.
- Laptops with cracked screens or liquid damage are replaced as a repair-or-replace decision by IT. Repeated damage may be referred to your line manager.
- **Lost or stolen devices are a security matter.** Follow the Lost or stolen device article instead and report it at once.

## Returning equipment

- Return unused equipment to the IT desk or by courier. Remote staff can request a prepaid return label through the helpdesk portal.
- Do not wipe the device yourself. IT erases and re-images every returned device.
- Return all equipment within 10 working days of a role change or your last working day. Unreturned items may be charged to your department.
- You will receive an email confirmation once IT has booked the item back in. Keep it until your asset record is cleared.

## Questions

Raise a ticket with the category **Equipment Request**, or ask at the IT desk.
