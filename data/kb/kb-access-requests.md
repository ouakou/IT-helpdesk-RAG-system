# Access requests and permission changes

You need approval to access anything beyond the standard set given to your role. IT cannot grant access on request alone, even to senior staff.

## How to request access

1. Raise a ticket with the category **Access Request**.
2. Say what you need access to (system, folder path or mailbox name), what level you need (read only, edit, admin) and why.
3. Name the person who owns the system or data, if you know.

## Approval

- Your **line manager** approves first.
- The **data owner** (the person responsible for that system or folder) approves second.
- Both are asked by email. Each has **3 working days** to respond; unanswered requests are chased once and then escalated.
- Access to Finance, HR or payroll systems also needs sign-off from that department's head.

After approval IT usually applies the change within 1 working day. You will get an email when it is done.

## Temporary access

State an end date in the ticket. Temporary access is removed automatically on that date. Access that is not used for 90 days is reviewed and may be removed.

## Changing role or team

When you move team, your manager raises a ticket for the new access. Old access is not removed automatically, so ask your previous manager to raise a removal request.

## Not allowed

Never share your login to give someone access, and do not ask a colleague to "borrow" their account. Both break the acceptable use policy.
