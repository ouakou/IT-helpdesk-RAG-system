# Ticket status and escalation process

## Checking your ticket

You can see your own tickets at `helpdesk.acme.example` under **My tickets**. Each shows its status, priority, updates and comments. You can only see tickets you raised or that were raised on your behalf. You cannot look at a colleague's ticket; ask them to share the details or have them add you as a watcher.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New | Received, not yet picked up |
| In progress | An analyst is working on it |
| Waiting for you | We need information from you; the SLA clock is paused |
| Waiting for third party | A vendor or approver has to act |
| Resolved | We think it is fixed; you have 3 working days to reopen |
| Closed | Finished |

Resolved tickets close automatically after 3 working days with no reply.

## Waiting for you

Check your email for the question. If you do not answer within 5 working days we send one reminder and then close the ticket. You can reopen it by replying.

## Chasing a ticket

1. Compare the age of the ticket with its target in the SLA article.
2. If it is **within** target, add a comment with any new information.
3. If it is **past** target, ring ext. 4000 and quote the ticket number (like `INC-104233`).

## Escalating

If a P2 or P3 ticket is overdue by more than a day, or the impact has grown, ask for it to be escalated. The helpdesk supervisor reviews it and may raise its priority. P1 incidents (security incidents and major outages) are handled 24 hours a day on the hotline, ext. 4999.

## Complaints

Send feedback to `it-feedback@acme.example`.
