# Slow network or internet performance

Before raising a ticket, run through these checks. They fix most slow-connection reports.

## Quick checks (2 minutes)

1. Open a speed test such as `speed.acme.example`. In the office you should see at least 50 Mbps; at home at least 10 Mbps for video calls.
2. Close big downloads, cloud backups and streaming in other tabs or devices.
3. Restart the browser, then restart the laptop if it has not been restarted for over a week.

## In the office

- Staff devices join the **Acme-Staff** network and sign in with a device certificate, with no key to type. If you are on **Acme-Guest**, you will see slower speeds and limited access; switch to Acme-Staff.
- Move closer to an access point, or use a network cable and docking station for video calls.

## At home

- Use the 5 GHz band on your router if it has one, and sit near the router.
- Disconnect other heavy users (consoles, streaming) during calls.
- On the VPN, only work traffic is sent through Acme. Use the VPN only when you need internal systems; see the VPN setup article.

## Teams and video calls

Turn off your camera in large meetings, close unused apps, and check **Settings, Devices** in Teams to confirm the right headset is selected.

## When to raise a ticket

Raise a ticket with the category **Network** if slowness lasts more than a day on several devices, or if one office area is affected. Include your location, the time, and the speed test result. If many colleagues report the same problem, it is likely an outage; check the Outages and incident communications article.
