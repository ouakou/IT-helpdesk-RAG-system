# VPN legacy client instructions

Use the Acme VPN to reach internal systems (file shares, the intranet and the ticket system) when you are not in an Acme office. This article explains how to install and use the Acme VPN Classic client, which is the supported client for all staff.

## Installing the client

1. Download **Acme VPN Classic 4.2** from the Software Centre (Windows) or the Self Service app (macOS).
2. Run the installer and accept the defaults. You need no admin rights on a company laptop.
3. Restart your laptop when the installer asks.

## Connecting

1. Open **Acme VPN Classic** and set the server to `vpn-legacy.acme.example`.
2. Enter your normal Acme username and password.
3. When prompted, enter the **6-digit code sent to your mobile phone by SMS**. The code expires after 5 minutes.
4. Wait for the status to show **Connected**. Your session lasts 8 hours; reconnect after that.

## Troubleshooting

- **No SMS code arrives:** check that your mobile number in the staff portal is correct, then choose **Resend code**. Codes can take up to 2 minutes.
- **"Authentication failed":** confirm your password works on webmail first. If your account is locked, see the Account lockout article.
- **Connected but nothing loads:** disconnect, wait 30 seconds and reconnect. If it persists, restart the laptop.
- **Client will not start on macOS:** open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, and allow the Acme VPN extension.

## Still stuck?

Raise a ticket in the helpdesk portal with the category **Network / VPN** and include any error message shown.
