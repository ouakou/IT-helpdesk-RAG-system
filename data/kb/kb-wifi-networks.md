# Wi-Fi: office and guest networks

Acme offices have two wireless networks.

## Staff network: Acme-Corp

1. Open the Wi-Fi list on your laptop and choose **Acme-Corp**.
2. Enter the network key. The current key is printed on the posters next to the lifts and changes every 6 months.
3. If prompted, accept the security certificate.

Phones and tablets that are not company-owned should not use Acme-Corp.

## Guest network: Acme-Visitors

Visitors can use **Acme-Visitors**. The key is available from reception. Guests only get internet access, not internal systems.

## Problems connecting

- **Wrong key:** check the key on the posters; keys are updated in March and September.
- **Connected but no internet:** forget the network, then reconnect.
- **Windows 7 or 8:** if the laptop cannot see Acme-Corp, ask the IT team on floor 3 to update your wireless driver.
- **Slow Wi-Fi:** move closer to an access point or use a network cable.

## Still stuck?

Walk over to the IT team on floor 3 or ring the helpdesk on ext. 2200.
