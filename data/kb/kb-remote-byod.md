# Remote working and BYOD policy

## Working remotely

- Use your company laptop. Connect to the VPN when you need internal systems (see the VPN setup article).
- Keep your home Wi-Fi password-protected and your router updated.
- Lock your screen when you step away (Windows+L or Ctrl+Cmd+Q).
- Do not let family or friends use your work laptop.
- Public Wi-Fi (cafes, trains) is allowed only with the VPN on.

## Personal phones and tablets (BYOD)

Personal phones and tablets may be used only for **email, calendar and Teams**, through the Outlook and Teams apps.

To enrol:
1. Install **Company Portal** and sign in with your Acme account.
2. Accept the app protection policy. It controls only the work apps and cannot see your personal photos or messages.
3. Set a device PIN or biometric lock, and keep the operating system up to date.

If the device is lost, IT can remove the work data only. See the Lost or stolen device article.

## What BYOD does not allow

- **Personal laptops cannot be used for work** or connect to the VPN.
- No work files saved to the personal device, personal email or personal cloud storage.
- Rooted or jailbroken devices are blocked.

## Working abroad

Tell your manager and IT **at least 10 working days** before. Some countries are blocked for security reasons and you may need a loan laptop.

## Support

IT supports company devices fully. For personal devices we can help only with the Company Portal and work apps.
