# Helpdesk SLAs, priorities and how to raise a ticket

## How to raise a ticket

- **Portal:** `helpdesk.acme.example`, choose a category and describe the problem.
- **Phone:** ext. 4000, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00.
- **IT desk:** floor 1, 09:00 to 16:00.
- **P1 incidents (security incidents and major outages):** ext. 4999, 24 hours (see the Suspected breach article).

Good tickets say what you were doing, the exact error, when it started, which device you use and how many people are affected. Screenshots help.

## Priorities and targets

| Priority | Meaning | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Security incident, or a service down for many people | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 High | One person cannot work, or a team is blocked | 1 hour | 1 working day |
| P3 Medium | Work is slowed but possible | 4 working hours | 3 working days |
| P4 Low | Request or question | 1 working day | 5 working days |

Targets run during working hours only, except P1.

## Priority is decided by IT

You suggest the priority but IT sets it from the impact. Marking everything as urgent slows down real emergencies. A password reset is a P4 because you can do it yourself (see the Password reset article).

## Requests that take longer

Equipment, software reviews and access approvals depend on third parties. Their timings are in their own articles and the SLA clock pauses while we wait for your reply.

## Out of hours

Outside hours, only P1 is handled, and you reach it on ext. 4999. Everything else waits for the next working day.
