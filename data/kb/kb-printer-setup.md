# Printer setup and common faults

Acme uses **follow-me printing**. You send a job once and collect it at any office printer by tapping your staff badge. Jobs are deleted after 24 hours if not collected.

## Adding the printer

**Windows:** Open Settings, Printers & scanners, Add device, and choose **ACME-PRINT** on `print.acme.example`. If it does not appear, install it from the Software Centre (search "Acme Printing").

**macOS:** Open Self Service and install **Acme Printing**. The printer is added automatically.

## Printing

1. Print to **ACME-PRINT** as normal.
2. At any printer, tap your badge and choose your jobs on the screen.
3. Press Print. Colour printing is off by default; choose it on the print screen if needed.

## Common faults

- **Job not showing at the printer:** check you printed to ACME-PRINT, not a personal printer. Wait a minute and refresh.
- **Badge not recognised:** register it once at `badge.acme.example`; new badges are registered by IT on day one.
- **Paper jam:** follow the screen prompts. Do not force doors open.
- **Out of paper or toner:** paper is in the supplies cupboard on each floor. For toner, raise a ticket with the printer ID (a label on the front, like PR-0312).
- **Printing from home:** not supported. Use PDF and print at the office.

## Raising a ticket

Raise a ticket with the category **Printing** and include the printer ID and what the screen says.
