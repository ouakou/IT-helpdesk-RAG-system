# Leavers and offboarding

When someone leaves Acme, their access must be removed on time and their equipment and data handled properly.

## For managers: before the last day

Raise a ticket with the category **Leaver** at least **5 working days** before the last working day. Include:

- the leaver's name and last working day
- who should receive their email and files (usually the manager)
- whether any shared mailboxes, licences or system accounts need reassigning
- the equipment that must be returned

For an unplanned or sensitive departure, ring the helpdesk on ext. 4000 so access can be removed immediately.

## What IT does

- On the **last day at 17:00** the account is disabled and sessions end.
- The mailbox is kept for **30 days** with automatic reply and forwarding if requested, then deleted.
- OneDrive files are transferred to the manager for **30 days** and then deleted.
- Licences are reclaimed and added to the pool.

## For the leaver

- Move work files from personal folders to a team location. Do not copy files off the device.
- Hand back all equipment within **10 working days** at the IT desk or by courier (see the Equipment requests article). Do not wipe the laptop.
- Remove the work accounts from your own phone. IT will wipe work data from BYOD devices.

## Contractors

Contractor accounts expire automatically on their end date, so the manager only needs to confirm the date or an extension.

## After the leaving date

Access is not restored for leavers. Rehires are treated as new starters (see the New starter article).
