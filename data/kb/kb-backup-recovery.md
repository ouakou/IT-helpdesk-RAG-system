# Backup and file recovery

Do not rely on your laptop's hard drive. Acme protects files saved in the right places and not those saved only on the device.

## What is backed up

| Location | Backup | How long |
|---|---|---|
| OneDrive (Desktop, Documents, Pictures are synced automatically) | Version history and recycle bin | 30 versions; deleted files 93 days |
| S: shared drive | Daily snapshots | 14 days |
| Email | Deleted items recoverable | 30 days |
| Files saved only on `C:\` or the macOS disk | **Not backed up** | |

## Recovering a file yourself

**OneDrive:** open `onedrive.acme.example`, choose **Recycle bin** to restore deleted files. For an older version of a file, right-click it, choose **Version history**, and restore.

**S: drive:** right-click the folder, choose Properties, then Previous Versions.

## Asking IT to restore

For files older than the periods above, or anything lost from a shared system, raise a ticket with the category **Data Recovery**. Include the full path, the date it was last good and who deleted it if known. Restores from the monthly archive take up to **3 working days**.

## Laptop failure

If your laptop dies, files in OneDrive are safe. IT will give you a replacement and sign you in to restore them. Anything stored only locally may be unrecoverable, so move it to OneDrive now.

## Do not use

USB sticks and personal cloud storage are not permitted for work files. See the Data protection article.
