# Data protection and handling sensitive data

Acme handles personal data about staff and customers. UK GDPR requires us to protect it, and breaches can be reported to the regulator within 72 hours.

## Data classes

| Class | Examples | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Marketing material | Share freely |
| Internal | Policies, org charts | Acme staff only |
| Confidential | Contracts, customer lists, salaries | Need-to-know; share by secure link, never as an email attachment to external addresses |
| Restricted | Passwords, medical, payment-card data | Only in approved systems; never in email or chat |

## Everyday rules

- Share via OneDrive or SharePoint links to named people (see the Shared drives article).
- **No USB sticks** or personal cloud storage for work files.
- Never put customer personal data in public AI tools or chatbots.
- Lock your screen and use a privacy screen on public transport.
- Print only what you need, and collect it straight away (see the Printer setup article).

## Requests for someone else's data

You must not ask IT for another person's files, emails, tickets or personal details unless your manager and HR have approved it in writing. IT will refuse such requests.

## Subject access requests

If a customer or colleague asks for a copy of their data, do not answer directly. Forward the request to `privacy@acme.example` on the same day.

## Suspected data breach

If you sent data to the wrong person or lost a device, report it **immediately** through the Suspected breach article. Do not wait to see whether it matters.
