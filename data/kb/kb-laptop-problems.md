# Laptop won't boot, slow or crashing

## Laptop will not turn on

1. Plug in the charger, wait 15 minutes, then try again. Check the light on the charger.
2. Hold the power button for 15 seconds, release, then press once to start.
3. Try a different power socket. Remove docking stations and USB devices.

If it still shows no sign of life, raise a ticket with the category **Hardware** and include the asset tag. Keep your files safe: do not open the laptop.

## Laptop starts but will not load Windows or macOS

If you see a black screen, a repair screen or a flashing question mark, do not reinstall the system yourself. Raise a ticket or visit the IT desk (floor 1, 09:00 to 16:00). Your files are normally saved in OneDrive, see the Backup and file recovery article.

## Laptop is slow

- Restart it. Many problems clear after a restart; **shut down fully at least once a week**.
- Check storage: if there is less than 10% free disk space, delete old downloads and empty the Recycle Bin.
- Close apps you do not use; open Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) to find one using lots of memory.
- Install pending updates (see the Software updates article).

## Crashes and blue screens

Note the error message or stop code and when it happened. A single crash after an update is usually harmless. If it happens more than twice in a week, raise a ticket and attach a photo of the message.

## Battery drains quickly

Batteries older than 3 years lose capacity. The IT desk can check battery health and replace it if it is below 60%.
