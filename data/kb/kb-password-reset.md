# Password reset and self-service portal

You can reset your own Acme password without contacting the helpdesk, as long as you have completed MFA enrolment (see the MFA setup article).

## Resetting a forgotten password

1. Go to `password.acme.example` from any device, including your phone.
2. Enter your Acme email address and choose **Forgot my password**.
3. Approve the sign-in request in the Authenticator app.
4. Choose a new password and confirm it.

The new password works for the laptop, email, Teams and the VPN within about 5 minutes. If your laptop is already logged in, lock and unlock it once connected to the network so it picks up the change.

## Password rules

- At least 14 characters. A passphrase of four or more words is recommended.
- Must not contain your name or username, and must differ from your last 12 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. You get an email reminder 14 days before.

## Changing a password you know

Press Ctrl+Alt+Delete on Windows and choose **Change a password**, or use `password.acme.example` and choose **Change password**.

## If self-service does not work

If you cannot use the Authenticator app (for example you lost your phone) or the portal says your account is locked, see the Account lockout and MFA setup articles. The helpdesk will verify your identity before resetting anything, and will never ask you to tell them your password.
