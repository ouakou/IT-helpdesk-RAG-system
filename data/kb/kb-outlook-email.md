# Outlook and email: mailbox full, shared mailboxes, calendar

## Mailbox full

Each mailbox holds **50 GB**. You get a warning at 45 GB and cannot send at 50 GB.

1. Empty Deleted Items and Junk Email.
2. Sort by size (Search, Filter, Size) and delete large attachments you do not need.
3. Move old email to **Online Archive**, which appears under your mailbox in Outlook. It does not count against your 50 GB.

Deleted items can be recovered for 30 days: right-click Deleted Items and choose **Recover deleted items**.

## Shared mailboxes

Shared mailboxes (like `finance-queries`) are used by teams.

- To get access, raise an Access Request; the mailbox owner and your manager approve (see the Access requests article).
- Once approved, wait up to **1 hour** for it to appear in Outlook automatically. Restart Outlook if it does not.
- To send as the shared mailbox, choose it in the **From** field.

## Calendar

- Add someone as a delegate: Outlook, File, Account Settings, **Delegate Access**.
- Meeting invites showing as "tentative": check the invite came from a known sender.
- A calendar showing the wrong time: set the time zone in Outlook options and on your laptop.

## Email not arriving or not sending

Check Junk Email first. If a colleague says they did not receive your email, check Sent Items and the bounce message. Messages over **25 MB** are rejected; share large files through OneDrive links.

## Phishing

Report suspicious emails with the **Report Phish** button. See the Reporting phishing article.
