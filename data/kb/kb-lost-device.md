# Lost or stolen device

A lost or stolen laptop or phone may expose Acme data, so report it **immediately**, even if you think you will find it. Do not wait until the next working day.

## Step 1: Report it

- Call the security hotline on **ext. 4999**, available 24 hours (external: 020 7946 0999).
- If you cannot reach it, raise a ticket as soon as you can, with the category **Security**, or ask a colleague to.
- State what was lost, the asset tag if you have it, when and where, and whether it was locked.

## Step 2: What IT does

- Locks or wipes the device remotely (company laptops and phones: full wipe; BYOD phones: work data only).
- Disables your sessions and may reset your password and MFA.
- Logs the incident, and reports to the regulator within 72 hours if personal data was involved.

## Step 3: If stolen

Report the theft to the police and get a crime reference number. Send the number to the helpdesk. Acme needs it for insurance.

## Replacement

Once reported, a replacement is arranged through the Equipment requests process, but your manager must confirm. Expect 1 to 3 working days for a laptop. Your files in OneDrive will be restored when you sign in.

## If you find it

Do not switch it back on. Hand it to the IT desk; it will be checked and re-imaged before use.

## Prevent it

Keep devices with you, locked in a bag or the boot, never visible in a car. Use a screen lock on all devices.
