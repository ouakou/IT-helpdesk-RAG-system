# Account lockout

Your account locks automatically after **5 failed sign-in attempts**. This protects you if someone is guessing your password.

## What happens

- The lock lasts **15 minutes**, then clears by itself.
- During the lock, correct passwords are also rejected, so do not keep retrying.

## What to do

1. Wait 15 minutes and sign in again with the right password.
2. If you do not remember the password, use the self-service portal at `password.acme.example`. Resetting your password also unlocks the account straight away. See the Password reset article.
3. If the portal itself says the account is locked and you cannot wait, call the helpdesk (ext. 4000) or raise a ticket with the category **Account**.

## Why does it keep locking?

Repeated lockouts usually come from an old password saved somewhere:

- a phone or tablet with email set up before your last password change
- a mapped network drive or saved credential on a second laptop
- the VPN client with a saved password

Update or remove the saved password on those devices. If it still happens, raise a ticket and mention which devices you use; IT can see where the failed attempts come from.

## Unexpected lockouts

If your account locks and you have not been signing in, report it as suspicious. See the Reporting phishing and Suspected breach articles.
